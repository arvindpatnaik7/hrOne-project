**Looked at and decided it is NOT a defect**
- `load_dotenv()` (default `override=False`): real environment variables still win over `.env`, as required.
- `IST = timezone(timedelta(hours=5, minutes=30))` is correct (India has no DST).
- Comparing `date` strings with `$gte`/`$lte` is correct because `YYYY-MM-DD` sorts lexicographically in date order.
- Endpoints are plain `def` (not `async`): FastAPI runs them in a thread pool, and PyMongo's client is thread-safe, so this is fine for concurrency.
- `doc.pop("_id", None)` after `insert_one` in `create_employee` is right (insert_one adds `_id` to the dict); I kept the idea in the serialiser.
- The `compute_*` helper *idea* is good and they are reused, but each needed fixing (rows 4-6) before being reused.
